# Epic: Downhill Blade Jam MVP

> **Status**: In Progress
> **Tier**: workflow minimal
> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md`

**Goal:** Bomb an open mountainside on inline skates, carving, pumping, grinding and gapping your way to the bottom, where the fastest line is a secret the mountain makes you earn.

**Scope:** the brief's six MVP features, one story each.

**Order:** the brief's Build order: movement prototype, grinding, first full run, speedrun layer, look and sound pass. The chase camera is cross-cutting and is tuned as playtests ask.
